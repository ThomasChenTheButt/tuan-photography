# Surface: design 2 home ("Pen and wash"), `design 2/wc-sketch/`

Scope: the whole design-2 home surface (map, dive into a place, cover gallery, guide page, Flights view, opening). Mode: Experience. Audience: people who meet his landscape photographs first and later plan to shoot the same places; EN and 繁中 equally.
Constraints (owner-pinned): light only; nothing written across a photograph except the bottom-left caption on the cover; Instagram tuan_1127 the only contact; every string in both languages; design 1's 3D books stand on the map as the guides; Taiwan never singled out except as the flights' origin; only his real photographs and facts.
Unresolved: the 2026 Germany cities; Thailand's airports (assumed); the other design-2 versions are not maintained.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A traveller's sketchbook map you travel across, not a page you scroll: the whole world in pen and wash with every place he has been, and choosing one dives into it until its photograph takes over. It refuses the hero-image-plus-scroll travel blog and the glossy map app.

OWN-WORLD: cold-press sketchbook paper; near-black fine-liner coastlines with a varying weight; hatching on mountains from real relief; light washes laid slightly off the lines (pale cerulean seas fading offshore, ochre and sap green land, a light vermilion-rose on his 21 countries); every country lettered in Alegreya Sans small capitals; Alegreya serif for names and captions; vermilion only for travelling heads and rings; railway sleepers, dashed pen roads and a small inked airliner for routes; square corners; words as controls.

STORY: The visitor sees all the places at once, hovers a book, chooses one, watches the map magnify into it, reads the place name rising large, sees the photograph dissolve in full screen, swipes through that place's photographs, scrolls down into the guide; or opens the small globe to replay his real journeys leg by leg. They follow him on Instagram.

FIRST VIEWPORT: after a ~7 s opening (globe turning under the title "Tuan, Through the Lens" / Tuan 的鏡頭之旅, unrolling into the map while the title glides to the centre, every flight drawing across the map then fading), the painted map fills the window edge to edge at 1440×900 with all 19 books standing at their places; tiny site name top-left; EN/中文 and Instagram top-right; zoom words bottom-right; the Flights globe bottom-left; a slim "Photographs 68" tab on the right edge. No header.

FORM: Pen-and-wash sketchbook, round-4 variant chosen by the owner (seed 0163a3f5 lineage, owner-steered through four rounds); code-led, no comp. Signature interactions: the dive (plate zoom, arrival name card, full-frame photo dissolve, cover gallery swipe, scroll to guide) and the Flights replay (legs in order, country kept in frame, plane / railway / road marks, ≤7 s at 1×, speeds Slow / 0.5× / 1× / 1.5× / 2×, finale highlight, idle flights radiating from Taipei together, far-side routes seen through the globe).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
