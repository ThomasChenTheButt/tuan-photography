# Opening run, 2026-10-08

Nine approved, two failed. The inherited 24 openings (aperture, contact, filmstrip, burst, letters, pullback and their versions) were not touched. The openings page now lists 33.

## Approved
- **fold**: the photograph mirrored on its own centre line, then the seam gives and the true half unfolds. Source: https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/dark/ . Borrowed: the strange symmetry of a picture folded on itself.
- **obscura**: the photograph upside down and dim, as on ground glass; one turn rights it and lights it. Source: https://www.abelardomorell.net/camera-obscura . Borrowed: the inverted, dim landscape righted in one turn.
- **outline**: the photograph whole except the mountain, left as bare paper; Aoraki fills its outline in one beat. Source: https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/the-leftovers-season-2/ . Borrowed: one missing shape that pulls the eye.
- **pane**: fogged glass with drops; one wipe and the view through the car window is there. Source: https://tympanus.net/Development/RainEffect/ . Borrowed: glass as a second surface, cleared in one wipe (no WebGL).
- **pixels**: flat colour blocks, the photo eight pixels wide, halving in hard steps until sharp. Source: https://www.felicityingram.com/ . Borrowed: a stepped, not eased, reveal.
- **register**: the print arrives with cyan, magenta and yellow plates out of register; they slide into alignment. Source: https://www.typohypergraphicobject.page/ . Borrowed: arriving by coming into register, not by fading.
- **ridgeline**: thin lines draw the ridge, horizon and window frame on paper; the paper dissolves into the photograph. Source: https://www.artofthetitle.com/title/north-by-northwest/ . Borrowed: abstraction that turns out to be the real picture.
- **silver**: a dull silver sheet tilts a few degrees and the photograph appears on it like a daguerreotype. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daguerreotype . Borrowed: the image appearing by angle.
- **projector**: a dark beat, then the photograph drops into the window like a slide into the gate, with a small bounce. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carousel_slide_projector . Borrowed: the gravity drop and mechanical settle.
