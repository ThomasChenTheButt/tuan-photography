# Taste file: what the owner has said, in his words where possible. Read before scouting, judging or building.

- He is a landscape photographer. The photograph is the entrance; the design must never upstage it. His biggest fear is the design upstaging the photographs.
- Round one (a slow pull-back from the summit): "It feels pretty boring. I want it more epic. I want it more like a photographer. Aesthetic-wise."
- He wants "epic, special, not normal" openings, "aesthetically eye drawing, different from other projects, different from boring projects".
- Of fifteen openings he cut, the five he kept are all quick, decisive, and about the camera, the film or his name: aperture, contact sheet, film strip, burst, letters. The cut ones were slow and soft (darkroom, exposure, ripple), or about maps, books, video and award-site tricks (orbit, route, book, reel, counter, stack, slices, flythrough), or heavy collage (mosaic, prints).
- He does not want his own premise to confine the scouts: look freely for what is eye-catching and unlike the usual site. The router, not the scouts, brings it back to his site.
- References are sources of qualities, never templates. He rejected a look for copying a site.
- Bilingual: any word that appears must exist in English and 繁體中文 (the page's language is on <html lang>).
