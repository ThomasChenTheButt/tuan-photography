# Excluded designs: already tried on this site. Do not propose, reuse or extend any of these.

## Survivors in experiment `opening` (reference only, do not build on them)
- aperture: a shutter blink, iris blades turn open from the centre while focus pulls sharp
- contact: every photograph as one contact sheet on black; one frame is chosen and grows to the window
- filmstrip: a strip of film with sprocket holes races past, slows, stops on the one frame, which grows
- burst: hard cuts between full-window photographs, faster and faster, then it holds
- letters: his name on paper is the only window onto the photograph; the letters grow until one stroke is the window
- pullback: the page arrives on the summit and pulls back until the whole photograph is there (he found this boring)

## Cut by the owner on 2026-10-07
- darkroom: black, red safelight, the print develops in the tray: grain, shapes, colour
- mosaic: one photograph pulls back to be one tile among all 93 that together form the opening photograph
- prints: physical prints dealt onto the paper, the last grows to the window
- exposure: blown-out white, exposure comes down stop by stop with light leaks
- negative: a colour negative in its film rebate flashes to positive

## Cut by the owner on 2026-10-08 (all ten of round two)
- reel: his own video clips cut hard, then the still
- orbit: the earth from space turning to New Zealand and falling to the photograph
- route: a pen line draws every journey on a paper map, small prints at each place, then dives in
- grow: scroll-driven: a small print opens to the window as the visitor scrolls
- book: a closed guidebook opens, the first plate is the photograph, the camera moves in
- stack: photographs pop up at the centre one after another, the last spreads to the edges
- counter: a number counts to 100 on paper, then the photograph wipes up from below
- slices: the photograph in tall slices rising one after another
- ripple: heat-haze distortion settling to sharp (SVG displacement)
- flythrough: a camera flies through nested photographs into the opening one

## Cut by the owner on 2026-10-08 (graph run one; only ridgeline's concept kept)
- fold: the photograph mirrored on its centre line, the seam gives and the true half unfolds
- obscura: upside down and dim as on ground glass, one turn rights and lights it
- outline: the photograph whole except the mountain, left as paper, which fills in one beat
- pane: fogged glass with drops, one wipe clears the view
- pixels: flat colour blocks halving in hard steps until sharp
- register: cyan, magenta and yellow plates out of register slide into alignment
- silver: a silver plate tilts and the photograph appears like a daguerreotype
- projector: a dark beat, the photograph drops into the gate like a slide, with a bounce
