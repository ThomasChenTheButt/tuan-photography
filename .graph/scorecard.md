# Scorecard, shared by the inspiration check and the manager review

Each item is PASS or FAIL with one sentence of reason. One FAIL on items 1 to 6 fails the whole thing.
Items 7 to 9 are graded 1 to 5; the manager fails anything scoring under 3 on any of them.

## Hard rules (the brief)
1. Nothing is ever written across the photograph: no word, number, logo or button over it, at any moment. Words may sit on paper or on a dark stage beside or before the photograph, never on it.
2. Light only: the page itself is warm paper. A dark stage during the opening is allowed; a dark theme for the page is not.
3. Ends exactly on the page's own cover: the lead photograph, object-fit cover, full window, so the stage can fade out with no visible change.
4. Not one of the 21 excluded designs in `.graph/excluded.md`, and not an extension or variant of one.
5. Buildable in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with the runtime in `design 1/js/opening.js`: no framework, no WebGL library, no font or map server. Offline SVG, canvas and the Web Animations API are fine.
6. Borrows qualities, never copies: the inspiration is a source of a quality (a rhythm, a material, a way of revealing), not a page to reproduce. The owner once rejected a look for copying a site.

## Graded (1 to 5)
7. Eye-catching: would a stranger stop? Is there one authored moment, not a generic fade or scroll reveal?
8. A photographer's opening: does it belong to someone who makes landscape photographs, and does the photograph win over the effect by the end?
9. Craft: timing with intent (3 to 8 seconds), one ease language, no jank at desktop and phone width, no console errors, and the hand-over to the page is seamless.
