Home, ten ways

Each one is a whole home page. Same paper, same photographs, a different way of showing that every photograph has a story.

  1. 01Facing pagesThe home page as an opened travel journal: each photograph on one page, its account on the facing page.
  2. 02Two roadsUnder every photograph the road forks: back to the trip it was made on, and on to the place.
  3. 03The caption growsOne photograph per screen; as it settles in the middle, its caption unfolds line by line into the whole account of how it was made, and folds back as you move on.
  4. 04The back of the printSix prints on the table; turn one over and its story is written on the back.
  5. 05One photograph, all the wayThe whole page is one photograph's account, set large: where, when, how, and where it leads.
  6. 06ExposureEach photograph beside its own exposure, set large: the making is the headline, the place name stays small.
  7. 07One at a timeOne photograph owns the screen on its own sheet of paper; the next is one flick away.
  8. 08Margin notesA field notebook: the photographs large in the main column, and beside each one, in the margin, its notes, staying level with it while it passes.
  9. 09The foldEach photograph is printed on a folded sheet; unfold it and its story is written inside.
  10. 10Same place, twiceFive places photographed twice, side by side, with the month and settings under each.
