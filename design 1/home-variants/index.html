Home, ten ways

Each one is a whole home page. Same paper, same photographs, a different way of showing that every photograph has a story.

Open one, look, come back, then give it a rank and a note. Everything you write is kept in this browser.

  1. 01 Facing pages The home page as an opened travel journal: each photograph on one page, its account on the facing page. Seen
  2. 02 Two roads Under every photograph the road forks: back to the trip it was made on, and on to the place. Seen
  3. 03 The caption grows One photograph per screen; as it settles in the middle, its caption unfolds line by line into the whole account of how it was made, and folds back as you move on. Seen
  4. 04 The back of the print Six prints on the table; turn one over and its story is written on the back. Seen
  5. 05 One photograph, all the way The whole page is one photograph's account, set large: where, when, how, and where it leads. Seen
  6. 06 Exposure Each photograph beside its own exposure, set large: the making is the headline, the place name stays small. Seen
  7. 07 One at a time One photograph owns the screen on its own sheet of paper; the next is one flick away. Seen
  8. 08 Margin notes A field notebook: the photographs large in the main column, and beside each one, in the margin, its notes, staying level with it while it passes. Seen
  9. 09 The fold Each photograph is printed on a folded sheet; unfold it and its story is written inside. Seen
  10. 10 Same place, twice Five places photographed twice, side by side, with the month and settings under each. Seen
