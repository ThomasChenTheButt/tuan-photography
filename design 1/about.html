Thomas Chen

Landscape photographer, Taiwan

I photograph the places I travel to, natural landscapes and cities both, and the photograph is always about the place: its scale and its light. I live in Taiwan and have been to 21 countries, the first of them Malaysia, when I was eight.

I go out for the hour when the light suits a place, and I'll wait for it. I leave myself out of the frame, and I'm not interested in spots that are famous only for being photographed.

Every trip starts before I leave, in planning documents: where each spot is, when the sun sets there, how to get there, what to eat nearby. Then I go and stand in those places. The guides here are written afterwards, from what actually happened. I walked every spot in them myself, and every photograph on this site is mine. Some spots get an exact pin; fragile ones are left as a general area on purpose.

Questions about a place or a photograph: write to me.
