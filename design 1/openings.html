Openings

Twenty-four ways the home page could begin. Open one; it plays once and the page is itself again. Add ?opening=name to the home page's address to see any of them.

  1. ApertureA shutter blink, then the iris blades turn open from the centre while the focus pulls sharp.
  2. Aperture 2Stop by stop: the iris opens in seven clicks, each a snap and a hold, like turning the ring.
  3. Aperture 3On the summit: six blades open from the mountain itself, not the middle of the window.
  4. Aperture 4Paper blades: the iris is cut from the page's own paper and opens on it.
  5. Contact sheetEvery photograph you have made, laid out as one contact sheet on black. One frame is chosen and fills the window.
  6. Contact sheet 2On paper: a printed contact sheet, each frame in its black film border.
  7. Contact sheet 3The dive: the whole sheet comes up, then the camera moves into the chosen frame.
  8. Contact sheet 4Strip by strip: the film is laid down row by row, a clay grease pencil rings one frame, and it grows.
  9. Film stripA strip of film races past with its sprocket holes, slows, and stops on the one frame.
  10. Film strip 2Vertical: the strip runs down the window, as film runs through a projector.
  11. Film strip 3Three strips at once; the outer two keep running the other way, the middle one lands on the frame.
  12. Film strip 4On paper: one strip on a slant across the page, and the frame straightens as it grows.
  13. BurstHard cuts between full-window photographs, faster and faster, like a burst, until it holds on the one.
  14. Burst 2Punch-in: each frame lands tight on a detail and eases out, so the cuts hit harder.
  15. Burst 3On the camera's screen: the burst plays small in the middle of the paper, then the last frame grows.
  16. Burst 4Strobe: a white flash between frames, like a flash firing at each one.
  17. LettersYour name on paper, the photograph showing only through the letters. The letters grow until one stroke is the whole window.
  18. Letters 2陳亮元 alone, large, and the camera goes into one stroke.
  19. Letters 3Each letter shows a different photograph of yours; one by one they turn to the summit.
  20. Letters 4The letters arrive one by one, each rising into place, then the name grows.
  21. Pull backThe first try: the page arrives on the summit and pulls back until the whole photograph is there.
  22. Pull back 2From the lake shore: it begins on the trees at the water's edge, not the summit.
  23. Pull back 3Three framings: three hard cuts, tight, closer to the whole, then the whole.
  24. Pull back 4With a turn: it begins tight and a little rotated, and straightens as it pulls back.
