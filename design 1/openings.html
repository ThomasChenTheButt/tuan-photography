Openings

Five ways the home page could begin. Open one; it plays once and the page is itself again. Add ?opening=name to the home page's address to see any of them.

  1. ApertureA shutter blink, then the iris blades turn open from the centre while the focus pulls sharp.
  2. Contact sheet 2On paper: a printed contact sheet, each frame in its black film border.
  3. Letters 4The letters arrive one by one, each rising into place, then the name grows.
  4. RidgelineOn empty paper, ink lines draw the photograph's own edges, the ridge of Aoraki, the horizon, the frame of the car window; then the paper splits along the drawn ridge, sky half up and land half down, and the lines turn out to have been the photograph all along, which fills the window. Source
  5. ShadowsA dozen torn paper scraps stand on one line. A light walks across and their shadows swing, long and leaning, until at one angle they meet as the ridge of Aoraki; in that instant the shadow is the photograph's own dark and the paper warms into its sky. Source
