Openings

Eleven ways the home page could begin. Open one; it plays once and the page is itself again. Add ?opening=name to the home page's address to see any of them.

  1. DarkroomBlack. Under a red safelight the print comes up in the tray: grain first, then shapes, then colour.
  2. ApertureA shutter blink, then the iris blades turn open from the centre while the focus pulls sharp.
  3. Contact sheetEvery photograph you have made, laid out as one contact sheet on black. One frame is chosen and fills the window.
  4. Film stripA strip of film races past with its sprocket holes, slows, and stops on the one frame.
  5. BurstHard cuts between full-window photographs, faster and faster, like a burst, until it holds on the one.
  6. MosaicOne photograph fills the window. It pulls back and is one tile among all your photographs, which together form the opening one.
  7. LettersYour name on paper, the photograph showing only through the letters. The letters grow until one stroke is the whole window.
  8. PrintsPrints are dealt onto the paper one after another. The last lands straight and grows to fill the window.
  9. ExposureWhite, blown out. The exposure comes down stop by stop and light leaks drift across until the photograph is right.
  10. NegativeThe frame as a colour negative in its film rebate. A flash, and it turns positive and fills the window.
  11. Pull backThe first try: the page arrives on the summit and pulls back until the whole photograph is there.
