# BRIEF: Journey, tuan photography 陳亮元

Interviewed 2026-10-18 (four questions in chat; four answered from his standing brief in
`CLAUDE.md`, which he wrote and keeps). His own words are quoted where he gave them.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** From his brief: "The Printed Travel Journal", a travel journal set like a printed
   magazine on warm paper; restraint, "photos talk; text stays quiet"; his biggest fear is the
   design upstaging the photographs. References he named for craft: Along Dusty Roads, The Common
   Wanderer.
2. **The journey, in his words.** "it should start as a whole, like people should know how many
   trips ive been to, how many country, the total amount of photograph, these should sort of be
   like the landing page information taking the viewers into my world". Then the trips (Journey is
   about time, `PRODUCT.md`); each trip opens on "The trip, along its route".
3. **Energy curve.** His pick: "Strong open, calm reading."
4. **The one moment.** His pick: "Planes leave Taiwan" (every flight takes off from home at once
   and lands across the world).
5. **What no other site does.** His pick: "Scrolling goes back in time": a large year counts
   backwards as you scroll, 2026 to 2010, and the map shows only what he had seen by then.
6. **How far from premium-minimal.** Premium-minimal, his standing brief (light only, square
   corners, words not icons, nothing written on a photograph).
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** His answer: "it depends, it should start as a whole". Read as:
   one map that stays with the visitor the whole way (the whole first, then the years as chapters
   beside it). No video flight.
8. **Assets.** His own 93 photographs and his real routes (`data/site.json`). No generated imagery:
   the site shows only what is his. No kie.ai key, no spend.

## Grammar (authored, a ninth one)

**Rewinding atlas.** Not filmic one-shot (no video, no film), not continuous world (that needs a
generated flight), not chaptered editorial as written (its hero bans media, and his hero is the
map). Its own structure:

- **Nav:** the site's own top bar (Gallery, Journey, Guides, About), plus a **year folio** in the
  map column that counts down as the chapters pass. Nothing else fixed.
- **Hero:** a title spread: "Journey", one sentence, three real totals (trips, countries,
  photographs) on paper, the world map beside them. The planes leave Taiwan once, on arrival.
- **Sequence:** one sticky map for the whole page; the text column scrolls years as chapters,
  newest first, each trip a quiet entry with its photographs.
- **Close:** 2010, the first trip; then the map plays forward to the present in one sweep and holds.
  The last line is running text, not a button.
- **Bans:** scrub, video, drift gradients, magnet, spotlight, cards, counters with any number that
  is not his.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Arrival | awe | every plane leaves Taiwan at once and lands across the world; the totals settle as they land |
| 2 The latest years | calm, recognition | 2026 and 2025: his newest trips, photographs at reading pace, the map holding |
| 3 The rewind | wistfulness | the folio counts back; countries he had not yet seen go pale; routes lift off the map |
| 4 The first trip | intimacy | 2010, Kota Kinabalu: the map almost empty, one line out of Taiwan |
| 5 Return | resolve | the map plays forward to now in one sweep and holds on the whole of it |

**The peak:** act 1. "Every plane he ever took left Taiwan at the same moment and landed all over the
world, and the numbers stopped when the last one landed." It gets the most room: a whole first
screen with nothing else moving.

**It's the site where** you scroll back through his years and watch the world un-travel itself,
back to his first trip, then fill up again.

**Authored silence:** none. The calm after the peak is reading, not empty scroll.
