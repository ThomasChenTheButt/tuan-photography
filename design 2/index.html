Design 2 · 五個版本

The home page as a map. Five versions to choose from; each opens the guide from a book. 首頁是一張地圖，五個版本，挑一個。

Round 4 · 第四輪

Watercolour, four ways. Full-screen map with every country named; the photo dissolves in full screen with a caption in the corner; swipe for more photos, scroll down for the guide; click the small globe for your journeys. 水彩的四種畫法。

  1. Watercolour, refined水彩・精修The one you liked, finished properly: lighter, finer, full screen. 你喜歡的那一版，更細緻、滿版。
  2. Wet in wet濕中濕Looser and more painterly: colours bleed into each other, the deep sea darkens. 更自由的畫法，顏色互相暈開。
  3. Pen and wash鋼筆淡彩A travel sketchbook: ink drawing first, light washes over it. 旅行速寫本：先畫線，再上淡彩。
  4. Two pigments藍與柿Only indigo and persimmon on washi paper: calm and graphic. 只用靛藍和柿色兩種顏料。

Round 3 · 第三輪

One flat map with every place at a glance, books on it, the plate's slow zoom, a soft photo reveal, Marked's type, and a small globe of his flights. Five map styles. 一張看得到所有地方的地圖，五種畫法。

  1. Blind emboss壓印紙White on white: the world pressed into paper, lit from one side. Only the photos have colour. 白紙上壓出來的世界，只有照片有顏色。
  2. Swiss relief瑞士地形圖The real mountains of the world, shaded by hand-atlas rules in soft daylight colours. 真實的山脈地形，像精緻的地圖冊。
  3. Ink and silk水墨The world in ink wash on pale paper, with a small red seal on every place. 水墨畫的世界地圖，每個地方一枚印章。
  4. Two-ink riso雙色印刷A risograph print in two overprinting inks: bold, graphic, joyful. 兩色孔版印刷的地圖，大膽又有趣。
  5. Field watercolour水彩A traveller's map painted in watercolour, lettered like a journal. 旅人手繪的水彩地圖。

Round 2 · 第二輪

After his notes on round 1: pick a place, the map zooms in, the cover photograph appears, the guide begins. 選一個地方，地圖放大，封面照片出現，攻略開始。

  1. The dive俯衝One beautiful globe, nothing in the way. Touch a place and fall into its photograph. 一顆漂亮的地球儀，點一個地方就掉進那張照片。
  2. Atlas plate地圖冊，第二版The circled atlas, corrected: no circles on the index, the plate dives into the photograph. 圈起來的地圖冊修正版。
  3. Marked spots標記的地點His reference, made light: a ring, a leader line, a caption, then the photograph. 他給的參考圖，做成淺色版。
  4. Globe and index地球儀與索引The globe kept with its books, photographs laid around it like prints on a desk. 地球儀加書，照片像相片攤在桌上。
  5. Flyover飛越The map seen from a window seat; choosing a place is a descent. 從機窗往下看的地圖，選一個地方就降落。

Round 1 · 第一輪

  1. Departures出發A daylight departure board: choose a flight, the map flies there and the book rises. 機場出發看板，選一班，地圖就飛過去。
  2. Saved places收藏的地點Your Google Maps idea, made calm and light: books stand on the map like pins, photos appear as you zoom in. 像 Google 地圖，書像圖釘站在地圖上。
  3. Flight lines航線An in-flight magazine route map: lines from Taipei to every place, the book waits at the end of each line. 機上雜誌的航線圖，從台北出發的每一條線。
  4. Desk globe地球儀A globe you turn with your hand, or let it spin and choose for you. 用手轉的地球儀，也可以讓它幫你選。
  5. Circled atlas圈起來的地圖冊A printed atlas and a pen: every photo is a stamp in the index, circled on the map. 印刷地圖冊加一支筆，每張照片都圈在地圖上。
