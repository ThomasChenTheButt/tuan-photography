Design 2 · 五個版本

The home page as a map. Five versions to choose from; each opens the guide from a book. 首頁是一張地圖，五個版本，挑一個。

  1. Departures出發A daylight departure board: choose a flight, the map flies there and the book rises. 機場出發看板，選一班，地圖就飛過去。
  2. Saved places收藏的地點Your Google Maps idea, made calm and light: books stand on the map like pins, photos appear as you zoom in. 像 Google 地圖，書像圖釘站在地圖上。
  3. Flight lines航線An in-flight magazine route map: lines from Taipei to every place, the book waits at the end of each line. 機上雜誌的航線圖，從台北出發的每一條線。
  4. Desk globe地球儀A globe you turn with your hand, or let it spin and choose for you. 用手轉的地球儀，也可以讓它幫你選。
  5. Circled atlas圈起來的地圖冊A printed atlas and a pen: every photo is a stamp in the index, circled on the map. 印刷地圖冊加一支筆，每張照片都圈在地圖上。
